## Duplication incident (important, honest record)
The publish endpoint is INSERT-ONLY and appends `-2`, `-3`, … on slug collision
(documented as expected behaviour in CLAUDE.md). Two mistakes created extra
duplicate rows that cannot be removed via the endpoint (no update/delete, and
this agent is scoped to the publish endpoint only):

1. The first publish (`run-20260905-kz-sept-window`) succeeded for 7 of 8
   articles but WITHOUT covers (images failed to download due to the 429), and
   the ranking article did not insert on that pass.
2. The corrective publish (`run-20260905-kz-sept-window-v2`) re-sent all 8 WITH
   covers and expanded bodies. A retry-detection bug then treated every base
   slug as "missing" (because successful inserts return suffixed slugs) and
   re-posted all 8 a second time, inserting a further duplicate set.

Net effect: each story now has several rows (base / -2 / -3 / -4 …). The
canonical, fully-compliant version of every story (cover + expanded body) is
live under the `-2` slugs (and `-4` for the ranking); these are the ones to keep.
The earlier cover-less rows and the extra retry rows are duplicates that the
portal owner may prune. The retry logic has been noted; no further publishes
were made after discovering the issue.
